Pairing your band

  1. Charge the band for a few minutes.
  2. Open SLIDE Connect and tap Get Started (or Devices > Pair a Device).
  3. Keep the band next to your phone and tap it when it appears.

If the band does not appear: make sure no other app is connected to it. In the other app, disconnect the band, then go to iPhone Settings > Bluetooth, tap the band and choose Forget This Device.

More than one band

Your iPhone uses one band at a time. Every band you pair stays saved under Devices. Tap Switch to This Band to change. The band gives a short buzz when it connects.

Find my band

Open Devices, tap your band, then tap Find. The band buzzes and flashes its light.

Apple Health

Turn on Settings > Sync to Apple Health. SLIDE Connect copies steps, energy, distance, workouts, heart rate, HRV, blood oxygen, breathing rate and sleep into the Health app.

Call and app alerts

Choose which alerts buzz your band in Devices > your band > Call & App Alerts. Your iPhone must also allow notifications for each app.

Health readings

SLIDE Connect and the band are not medical devices. Readings are estimates for general wellness and are not meant to diagnose or treat any condition.
