SLIDE Connect

The companion app for your SLIDE fitness band
SupportPrivacy Policy

SLIDE Connect pairs with the SLIDE screenless fitness band (model SB138, also sold as KH138) and turns what it measures into a clear daily picture.

  • Activity: steps, distance and calories, hour by hour, with daily goals.
  • Heart: heart rate, resting heart rate, HRV, blood oxygen, stress and breathing rate, measured automatically through the day.
  • Sleep: deep, light, REM and awake stages with a nightly sleep score.
  • Readiness and PAI: see how well you recovered and your daily Vitality Index.
  • Workouts: workouts from the band, plus GPS runs, walks, hikes and rides with a map.
  • On your wrist: silent alarms, move and water reminders, call and app alerts, Do Not Disturb.
  • Apple Health: copy your data into the Health app.
